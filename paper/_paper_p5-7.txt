5. Summary 
With the vigorous development of Chinese stock market and the introduction of the idea of quantitative investment, 
more and more investors and related scholars began to pay attention to quantitative investment. However, the

development of quantitative investment in China is still in the initial stage, and many methods cannot be fully applied 
in this field. So, this paper tries to apply LightGBM algorithm to quantify investment.  
In this paper, we select five categories of indicator s that can affect the stock return: valuation, profit, scale, risk, 
and growth. We use three algorithms of random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM for rolling test, and find that 
LightGBM algorithm has the best effect. Subsequently, the Bayesian optimization m ethod was used to adjust the 
parameters, and the results showed that the Bayesian optimization method could effectively improve the algorithm 
performance, and again verified that the effect of LightGBM was the best. After selecting the multi factor stock 
selection model based on LightGBM-bayes, this paper uses the processed data to back test, selects the stock portfolio 
strategy containing 10 stocks, and calculates five investment strategy evaluation indicators. The results show that the 
indicators of the p ortfolio strategy selected by this model are better than the benchmark CSI 300 index. At present, 
the idea of quantitative investment has been widely used in China's stock investment market, but the integrated 
algorithm has not been widely introduced into the field of quantitative investment. The research results of this paper 
show that the multi factor stock selection model based on LightGBM-bayes is efficient and profitable and can provide 
a new method and idea for the future research of quantitative investment. 
However, there are still shortcomings in this paper. In future research, macroeconomic factors, such as GDP, should 
be added; In addition, during rolling test, the time interval shall be shortened and rolling test shall be conducted in 
quarterly units.
