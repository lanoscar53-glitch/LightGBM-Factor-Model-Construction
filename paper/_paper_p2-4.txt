Abstract 
With the development of the stock market, the number of individual investors has become more and more. Because of emotional 
and irrational factors, the ris k and instability of stock market  in China have been greatly increased. Therefore, it is necessary to 
introduce the idea of quantitative investment into the financial field.  In this paper, firstly we use random forest , XGBoost and 
LightGBM to conduct rolling tests on multiple factors. After parameter adjustment  based on Bayesian Optimization, we find that 
LightGBM-Bayes has the best effect. Finally, this paper uses the multi factor stock selection model based on LightGBM-Bayes to 
conduct a rolling back test on CSI300 Constituent Stocks, and the back test results are better than the benchmark CSI300 index.

1. Introduction 
In the 1990s, the establishment of Shanghai Stock Exchange and Shenzhen Stock Exchange marked the formal 
opening of Chinese securities market. With the vigorous development of Chinese economic market, relevant laws and 
regulations have become more and more perfect, and the securities market has been greatly assisted in its development. 
In September 2021, the Beijing stock exch ange was established, focusing on promoting the financing activities of 
small and medium-sized innovative enterprises, aiming to inject more vitality into Chinese securities market. With the 
development of the capital market, more and more ordinary people begin to "speculate in stocks", which constitutes a

major feature of Chin ese securities market: the number of individual investors is far more than that of institutional 
investors. However, problems also follow. Individual investors' irrational, emotional and subjective judgments have 
greatly increased the risks and instability of Chinese securities market. Therefore, at this stage, more and more scholars 
and researchers have introduced the concept of quantitative investment into Chin ese financial market an alysis. 
Quantitative investment can effectively avoid the irrational and emotional behavior of individual investors, and help 
investors find effective investment paths in a large number of relevant data to obtain high returns.  
Due to the late start of quantitative investment research in China, at present, scholars have not fully applied machine 
learning and other methods to quantitative stock selection, often using support vector machines, neural networks, and 
other methods. However,  XGBoost, LightGBM and o ther integrated algorithms have not been fully applied . For 
example, Zhong et al. used PCA to convert the data, and used DNN and ANN respectively for training, testing and 
prediction. The results showed that the data after PCA conversion was better, and DNN was better than ANN  [1]. 
Hoseinzade et al. compared the effect of deep CNN with that of shallow ANN and found that the effect of deep CNN 
was more significant and profitable in most tests  [2]. Zhou et al. combined short -term memory neural network and 
convolutional neural network to train and forecast the Chinese stock market [3]. Long et al. put forward an innovative 
model - multi filter neural network. They selected the extreme markets in the Shanghai Shenzhen 300 Index for training 
and forecasting. Finally, they found that the multi filter neural network has higher accuracy and stability and stronger 
profitability than traditional machine learning or single neural network models  [4]. Li Wenxing et al. used K -means 
to conduct multi factor stock selection and verified that the return rate of stock portfolio is ideal [5]. Wang Lun and Li 
Lu proposed a multi factor quantitative stock selection model based on deep forest and pointed out that this model has 
great advantages when the stock market goes up [6]. Wang Bingyu et al. used logical regression and SVM to forecast 
the stock price, and the stock portfolio obtained can obtain a higher income than that of CSI 300 in the same period  
[7]. These methods have all been used when quantifying stock selection . However, q uantification has very strict 
requirements for accuracy and time -consuming. The above methods cannot effectively guarantee the accuracy and 
timeliness of quantification. Therefore, this paper will use three models, random forest, XGBoost and LightGBM, to 
conduct rolling tests respectively, compare the accuracy of the models, and conduct Bayesian Optimization on the 
three algorithms to improve the algorithm performance and test speed. Finally, the algorithm with the best effect will 
be selected for stock selection in the back testing and the back testing results will be analyzed.  
The contents of the paper are as follows . Section two constructs the factor pool and preprocesses data. In section 
three, Bayesian optimization is carried out for the three algorithms, and the effects before and after optimization are 
compared. In section four, this paper uses the processed data set and the optimal stock selection model for back testing 
to prove the effectiveness of the model . Section five summarizes the whole paper and puts forward the shortcomings 
and prospects. 
2. Constructing factor pool and data preprocessing 
The stock pool selected in this paper is all A-share, with a time span of 10 years from January 1, 2012, to December 
31, 2021, including complete  bear market, bull market and shock market. The data source of this article is Wind 
financial terminal. After data preprocessing, there were 544 stocks and 13 factors.  
2.1. Constructing factor pool 
After referring to relevant literature [8-10], this paper focuses on the factor indicators that can effectively affect the 
stock return, which is also a common indicator system for institutional investors. The factors selected in this paper 
can be divided into five categories: valuation, profit, gro wth, scale, and risk. All factor data are from Wind financial 
terminal.

2.2. data preprocessing 
To improve the accuracy and interpretability of the integration algorithm, all factors need to be preprocessed before 
the model starts. Since the stock pool selected in this paper is all A-share, the first thing to do is to remove ST and ST* 
stocks. Because all the data in this paper are from the wind financial terminal, its database is authoritative and has a 
low error rate. To follow the fluctuation of the stock market, this paper will not deal with abnormal values of the data, 
but only deal with missing values and standardized processing. Finally, the data is labeled, and the current annualized 
rate of return is used as the benchmark. If the annualized rate of return is negative, the label is 0; When the annualized 
rate of return is positive, the label is 1. The final data is 554 stocks and 13 factors. 
3. Algorithm selection and optimization 
In the third part, the data set that can be directly trained has been obtained. From January 1, 2012, to December 31, 
2021, there are ten years in total. From 2012, the data of the first five years are used as the training set, and the data 
of the sixth yea r are used for the rolling test.  According to the confusion matrix, this paper calculates the precision, 
recall, accuracy, and AUC values respectively, and compares the test results of the three algorithms.  The schematic 
diagram of rolling test is as follows:

3.1. Comparison of algorithm effects 
Since the modeling in this paper is not a single modeling, but a periodic and continuous modeling process, after 
five cross verifications, the average value of the evaluation indicators of these three algorithms is calculated to judge

the effect of the algorithm, as shown in Table 2. This result can not only represent the accuracy of the algorithm, but 
also reflect whether it has stability. 
Table 2. Algorithm effect before parameter adjustment 
 Precision Recall Accuracy AUC 
Random Forest 0.7124 0.9994 0.5974 0.9671 
XGBoost 0.7279 0.9997 0.7942 0.9872 
LightGBM 0.7452 0.9995 0.8305 0.9860 
As shown in Table 2, LightGBM has the best effect in terms of accuracy and accuracy, followed by XGBoost. The 
AUC values of the three algorithms are all above 0.9. Although LightGBM is slightly 0.1% lower than XGBoost in 
recall and AUC values, the accuracy of LightGBM is 4% higher than XGBoost, and the operation speed and efficiency 
of LightGBM are unmatched by XGBoost, and the focus of quantitative investment is on "fast" and "accurate", so this 
paper considers that the LightGBM algorithm before parameter adjustment has the best effect.

algorithm, selects the average of precision, recall, accuracy, and AUC values as the judgment criteria, and iterates 200
